Most component states are checked live from your own browser against each service directly, so this page cannot report a service as healthy when your network cannot reach it. The MCP endpoint is the exception: a browser cannot check it without logging an error for a reply that is actually correct, so it is reported from the external probe instead, with the time of that check shown. Uptime bars and the response history come from an independent probe that runs every 30 minutes outside our infrastructure, and days carrying a recorded incident are counted from that incident rather than from the probe, because the probe cannot report an outage it was not running for. A day we have no information about is drawn grey and left out of the percentage. Incident notes are written by hand.
